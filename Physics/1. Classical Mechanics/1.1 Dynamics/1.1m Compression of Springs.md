>[!note] Hooke's Law
>**Hooke’s Law** states that the force required to extend or compress a spring by some distance is proportional to that distance. Mathematically, it is expressed as
>$$F_S = -kx,$$
>where $F_S$ is the restoring force exerted by the spring, $x$ is the displacement of the spring from equilibrium, and $k$ (in N/m) is the spring constant, which measures the stiffness of the spring.
