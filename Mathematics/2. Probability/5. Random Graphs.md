>[!note] Erdos-Renyi Random Graph
>Given a positive integer $n \in \mathbb{Z}_{+}$ and a probability value $p \in [0,1]$, an **Erdos-Renyi random graph** $\mathcal{G}(n,p)$ is an [[3.1 Graph|undirected graph]] on $n$ vertices, such that each of the $\binom{n}{2}$ edges is present independently based on some [[2.2b.2 Bernoulli Distribution|Bernoulli random variable]] $P \sim \text{Ber}(p(n))$. Let $\lambda > 0$ be some constant for which
>$$p(n)=\lambda \frac{\ln n}{n}.$$
>If $\lambda < 1$, then $\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{G}(n,p) \text{ is connected}) \to 0$. However, if $\lambda > 1$, then $\mathbb{P}(\mathcal{G}(n,p) \text{ is connected}) \to 1$.

>[!example] II. Random Bipartite Graph
>Consider a random bipartite graph with $K$ left nodes and $M$ right nodes. Each of the $K \cdot M$ possible edges of this graph is present independently with probability $p$.
>
>**A)** Find the distribution of the degree of a particular right node.
>>[!example]- A) Solution.
>>Let $D_{m}$ be the randomly distributed degree of some right node $m$. Since the graph is bipartite, $m$ can only connect to a maximum of $K$ left nodes. Given that each edge is independently distributed As such, $D_{m} \sim \operatorname{Bin}(K, p)$.
>
>**B)** Now fix a left node $u$ and right node $v$. Conditioned on the event that the edge $(u,v)$ is present, find the distribution of the degree of $v$.
>>[!example]- B) Solution.
>>Let $D_{v} \sim \operatorname{Ber}(K, p)$ be the random variable representing the degree of vertex $v$, and let $E \sim \operatorname{Ber}(p)$ be the indicator of whether $(u, v)$ exists. Then,
>>$$\begin{align}
\mathbb{P}(D_{v}=d_{v} \mid E=1)&=\frac{\mathbb{P}(E=1 \mid D_{v}=d_{v})\mathbb{P}(D_{v}=d_{v})}{\mathbb{P}(E=1)} \\
&= \frac{1}{p}\mathbb{P}(E=1 \mid D_{v}=d_{v})\mathbb{P}(D_{v}=d_{v}) \\
&= \frac{d_{v}}{pK}\binom{K}{d_{v}}p^{d_{v}}(1-p)
^{K-d_{v}} \\
&= \binom{K-1}{d_{v}-1}p^{d_{v}-1}(1-p)^{K-d_{v}},
\end{align}$$
>>so $D_{v} \sim \operatorname{Bin}(K-1, p)+1$.
>

>>$$\mathbb{P}(D_{v} = 1)= K \cdot p(1-p)^{K-1}.$$
