>[!note] Game of Incomplete Information (Bayesian Game)
>A Bayesian game is a game in which players do not know some relevant characteristics of their opponents, which may include: 
>- Their payoffs.
>- Their available options.
>- Their beliefs.
>
>In a Bayesian game, player $i \in N$'s preferences are represented by a set $T_i$ known as $i$'s **type space**, known by all players in the game. Each element $t_i \in T_i$ is a single **type** of player $i$, not known by any of the other players $j \in N \backslash i$.
>
>Furthermore, a player's strategy gives a choice of some $s_i \in S_i$ for each type $t_i \in T_i$.
