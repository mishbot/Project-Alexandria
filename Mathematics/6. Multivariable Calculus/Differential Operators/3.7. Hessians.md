[!note] Hessian
>The **Hessian** of a scalar-valued function $f : \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ is the **square matrix of second-order partial derivatives** of $f$ with respect to the components of its input vector $x = [x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_n]^T$. It characterizes the **local curvature** of $f$ at a given point $x$ and is defined as
>$$H_{f}=\nabla^2f(x) = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1^2} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_1 \partial x_n} \\ \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n \partial x_1} & \cdots & \frac{\partial^2 f}{\partial x_n^2} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}.$$
>The Hessian indicates the **directions and magnitudes of curvature**, determining whether $f$ curves upward, downward, or remains flat near $x$.
