>[!note] Quadratic Program
>A **Quadratic Program (QP)** is an [[1. Standard Form Optimization|optimization problem]] where the objective function is quadratic, and the constraints are affine. Its **standard form** is given as
>$$\begin{aligned} p^* = \min_\limits{x} \quad & \frac{1}{2}x^THx+c^Tx \\ \text{s.t.:} \quad & Ax \leq b, \end{aligned}$$
>where $f_{0}(x)=\langle c, x \rangle$ for some decision variable $x \in \mathbb{R}^n$ and constants $H \in \mathbb{S}^n$ (and $H \in \mathbb{S}^n_{+}$ for the QP to be convex), $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, $b \in \mathbb{R}^m$, $c \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
