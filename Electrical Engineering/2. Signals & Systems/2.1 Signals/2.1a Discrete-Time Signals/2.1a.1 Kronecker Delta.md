>[!note] Kronecker Delta (Discrete-Time Unit Impulse)
>The **kronecker delta** (i.e. the discrete-time unit impulse) is defined as
>$$\delta[n]=\begin{cases}0 && \text{if } n=0 \\ 1 && \text{if } n \neq 0\end{cases}.$$
>Any arbitrary discrete-time signal $x(n)$ can be decomposed into a linear combination of discrete-time impulses (Kronecker deltas), such that
>$$x[n] = \sum^{\infty}_{k=-\infty}x[k]\delta[n-k].$$

# Properties
#### (Step Representation) $\delta[n] = u[n] - u[n-1]$.
The RHS is $1$ only at $n=0$. Any value $n < 0$ will lead to $0-0=0$, whereas any value $n > 0$ will lead to $1-1=0$.
