>[!note] Linear Filters
>A **linear filter** modifies an image by applying a **weighted sum of pixel intensities** in a local neighborhood. The same operation is repeated across all pixels, making it **shift-invariant** and **linear**. Mathematically, the operation we are performing here is called a **convolution**:
>$$I’(x, y) = \sum_{u,v} K(u,v) \, I(x - u, y - v),$$
>where K is the **kernel** (filter mask) and I’ is the filtered image.
>
>Linear filters have various uses:
>- Edge detection, using **finite difference operators** or the **Sobel filter**.
>- Blurring using a **box filter** or a **Gaussian blur**.
>- Sharpening using a **Laplacian filter**.
