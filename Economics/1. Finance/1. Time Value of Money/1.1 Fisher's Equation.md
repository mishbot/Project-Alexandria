>[!note] Fisher's Equation
>**Fisher’s equation** is an economic identity that shows that the nominal interest rate $i$ reflects both the real return on an investment $r$ and the loss of purchasing power due to inflation, $\pi$. Formally, it is expressed as
>$$1+i = (1 + \pi)(1 + r),$$
>and is often approximated as $i=\pi+r$.
