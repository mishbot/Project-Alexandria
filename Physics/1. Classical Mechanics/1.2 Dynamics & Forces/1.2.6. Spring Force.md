>[!note] Spring Force
>**Spring force** is the restoring force exerted by a spring when it is compressed or stretched from its equilibrium position. It acts in the direction opposite to the displacement and is given by **Hooke’s Law**, such that
>$$\vec{F}_s = -k\vec{x},$$
>where $\vec{F}_s$ is the spring force, $\vec{x}$ is the displacement from equilibrium, and $k$ is the spring constant (in N/m), which quantifies the stiffness of the spring. The negative sign indicates that the force always acts to restore the spring to equilibrium.
