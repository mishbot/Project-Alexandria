>[!note] Kinetic Energy
>Kinetic energy is the energy an object possesses due to its motion. It depends on both the object’s mass and the speed at which it’s moving, and is given by the formula
>$$K=\frac{1}{2}mv^2,$$
>where $v$ is the average velocity of the object during the time frame.
>

# Intuition
Consider a car traveling over a distance $x$. Its acceleration during that time can be modeled as $a = \frac{v_f^2 - v_i^2}{2d}$ from the motion equation $v_f^2 = v_i^2 + 2ad$. Then, we determine the kinetic energy using $W = Fd$. Since all of the force applied on the car is used to drive forward, there is no need to take the force's parallel component.

The above equation can be simplified to $W=mad$ to get
$$W = m\left(\frac{v_f^2 - v_i^2}{2d}\right)d=\cfrac{1}{2}mv_f^2-\cfrac{1}{2}mv_i^2.$$
