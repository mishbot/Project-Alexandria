>[!note] Business Entities
>**Business entities** are legal structures through which commercial activities are conducted and organized, each offering distinct liability protections, tax treatments, and ownership rules. They can range from simple, single-owner operations to complex corporations with multiple shareholders, including (but not exhausting):
>- **Sole Proprietorship**: A simple structure owned by one person who is personally liable and reports profits on their personal tax return.
>- **Partnership**: An arrangement where owners share profits, are personally liable, and benefit from pass-through taxation.
>- **LLC (Limited Liability Company)**: A hybrid structure offering limited personal liability, flexible management, and pass-through taxation by default.
>- **C Corporation (C Corp)**: A separate legal entity with limited liability for owners, but subject to double taxation at the corporate and shareholder levels.
>- **S Corporation (S Corp)**: A corporation with limited liability, pass-through taxation, a limit of 100 shareholders, and certain ownership restrictions.
>- **Nonprofit Corporation**: A tax-exempt entity formed for charitable, religious, or public service purposes, where no individual is personally liable.
