>[!note] Hockey-Stick Identity
>The **Hockey-Stick Identity** is a property of the binomial coefficient, stating that the sum of entries along a diagonal in Pascal’s triangle equals the entry directly below and one place to the right of the last term in the sum.
>
>Formally, for integers $n \ge r \ge 0$, the identity is expressed as
>$$\binom{n}{k+1} = \sum^{n-k}_{i=1} \binom{n-i}{k}.$$
