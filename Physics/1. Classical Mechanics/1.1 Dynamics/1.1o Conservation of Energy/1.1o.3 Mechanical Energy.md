>[!note] Mechanical Energy
>The **total mechanical energy** of a conserved system can be defined as the sum of the kinetic energy plus the potential energy of the system at any moment, such that
>$$E=K_\text{net}+U_\text{net} \iff K_1 + U_1 = K_2 + U_2 \iff E_1 = E_2.$$
>The definition of mechanical energy explicitly expresses that it is a conserved quantity.
