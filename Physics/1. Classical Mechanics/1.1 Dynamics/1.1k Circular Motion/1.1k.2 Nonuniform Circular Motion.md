>[!note] Nonuniform Circular Motion
>Nonuniform circular motion occurs when an object moves along a circular path with a changing speed. Unlike [[1.1k.1 Uniform Circular Motion|uniform circular motion]], where the speed remains constant, nonuniform circular motion involves variations in the magnitude of the velocity, though the direction of motion is still continuously changing as the object follows the circular path:
>```tikz
>\usepackage{amsmath}
>
>\begin{document}
>
>\begin{tikzpicture}
>
>    % First graph: Forces (left side)
>    \begin{scope}[shift={(-2.5,0)}, rotate=90]
>        % Circle & Radius
>        \draw[dashed,thick] (0,0) circle (2);
>        
>        % Force vectors
>        \draw[->, thick, magenta] (2,0) -- +(0,-1) node[right] {$\vec{F}_{\text{tan}}$}; % Tangential force
>        \draw[->, thick, magenta] (2,0) -- +(-0.925,-0.925) node[below] {$\vec{F}$}; % Radial force
>        \draw[->, thick, magenta] (2,0) -- +(-1,0) node[below right] {$\vec{F}_R$}; % Total force
>        
>        % Labels
>        \node at (2,0) [circle,fill,inner sep=1.5pt] {}; % point on the circle
>    \end{scope}
>    
>    % Second graph: Accelerations (right side)
>    \begin{scope}[shift={(2.5,0)}, rotate=90]
>        % Circle
>        \draw[dashed,thick] (0,0) circle (2);
>        
>        % Acceleration vectors
>        \draw[->, thick, orange] (2,0) -- +(0,-1) node[right] {$\vec{a}_{\text{tan}}$}; % Tangential acceleration
>        \draw[->, thick, orange] (2,0) -- +(-0.925,-0.925) node[below] {$\vec{a}$}; % Total acceleration
>        \draw[->, thick, orange] (2,0) -- +(-1,0) node[below right] {$\vec{a}_R$}; % Radial acceleration
>        
>        % Labels
>        \node at (2,0) [circle,fill,inner sep=1.5pt] {}; % point on the circle
>    \end{scope}
>
>\end{tikzpicture}
>
>\end{document}
>```
>Since acceleration has more than one direction, so does force. Therefore,
>$$\vec{a} = \vec{a}_R + \vec{a}_{\tan} \implies |\vec{a}| = \sqrt{|\vec{a}_R|^2 + |\vec{a}_{\tan}|^2}$$
>where $a_R = \frac{|\vec{v}^2|}{r}$ and $a_{\tan} = \frac{d\vec{v}}{dt}$.
