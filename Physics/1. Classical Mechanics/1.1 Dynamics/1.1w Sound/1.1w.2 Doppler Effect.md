>[!note] Doppler Effect
>The **Doppler Effect** is the apparent change in the frequency (or wavelength) of a wave as observed by an observer moving relative to the source of the wave. This effect occurs for all types of waves, including sound, light, and water waves. For a sound wave, the observed frequency $f{\prime}$ is given by:
>$$f\prime = \frac{v \pm v_{O}}{v \pm v_{S}}f_{S},$$
>where $\pm v_{O}$ is the speed of the observer (positive if moving toward the source), while $\pm v_{s}$ is the speed of the source (positive if moving toward the observer). The frequency $f_{S}$ is the source frequency.
