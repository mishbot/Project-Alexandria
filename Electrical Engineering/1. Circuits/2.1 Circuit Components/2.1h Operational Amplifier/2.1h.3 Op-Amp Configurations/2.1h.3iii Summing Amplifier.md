>[!note] Summing Amplifier
>A summing amplifier is an [[2.1h.1 Ideal Op-Amp|op-amp]] configuration that adds two voltages together in addition to adding a gain to the output voltage, such that $V_{o}=G_{1}V_{1}+G_{2}V_{2}$.
>
