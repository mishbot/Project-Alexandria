# Differential Kinematics

While [[2.1. Forward Kinematics|forward kinematics]] maps joint angles to end-effector poses, **differential kinematics** studies how joint *velocities* map to end-effector *velocities*. This linearization of the kinematic map is captured by the Jacobian matrix, which is central to velocity control, singularity analysis, and numerical inverse kinematics.

- [[5.1. Rigid Body Velocity]] — Angular velocity (spatial/body), full rigid body velocity ($V^s$, $V^b$), the adjoint map, velocity composition
