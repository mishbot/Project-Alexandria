>[!note] Path-Dependent Options
>A **path-dependent option** has a payoff at expiry $T$ that uses prices observed before $T$ and the final price $S_T$. The contract fixes the dates on which the price is observed. Three families are common, one per note.
>- [[2.3.6.2a Barrier Options]] covers options that a touch of a barrier $B$ cancels or activates. A knock-out and the matching knock-in add up to the vanilla option.
>- [[2.3.6.2b Asian Options]] covers options that use the average $A_T$ of the observed prices. Shares and cash replicate the average price call minus the put.
>- [[2.3.6.2c Lookback Options]] covers options that use the highest observed price $M_T$ or the lowest $m_T$.
>
>A knock-out and an Asian option cost less than the vanilla, and a lookback costs more. All three notes come from IEOR 221 Module 9 and its discussion section.

# Contents

- [[2.3.6.2a Barrier Options]]: the four single-barrier calls, regular and reverse barriers, in-out parity, monitoring, rebates, double barriers and barrier types, barrier bending, vega, skew and term structure, static hedging, and uses including reverse convertible notes. **Not taught:** Hull's closed forms for the down-and-in call and put; the discrete-monitoring shift; the exact static hedge.
- [[2.3.6.2b Asian Options]]: average price and average strike payoffs, why averaging lowers the price, the geometric average bound and the control variate, uses, and Asian parity with its Greeks. **Not taught:** the geometric Asian closed form; Asian parity with dividends or after some fixings; the identity $c_1 + c_2 - c_3 = p_1 + p_2 - p_3$ (homework only).
- [[2.3.6.2c Lookback Options]]: floating and fixed strike payoffs, why lookbacks cost more, uses, and the vega of the range $M_T - m_T$. **Not taught:** lookback pricing on a tree; the link between fixed and floating strike lookbacks.

Read the notes in order. 2.3.6.2c compares the lookback range with the Asian parity pair of 2.3.6.2b. Read 2.3.6.2a before [[2.3.6.3b Autocallables]] and [[2.3.6.3e Reverse Convertible Notes]], which embed its down-and-in put. Readers short on time should read 2.3.6.2a.
