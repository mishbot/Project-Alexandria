>[!note] Shamir's Secret Sharing Scheme
>**Shamir’s Secret Sharing Scheme** is a method for dividing a secret among a group of people in such a way that only groups of above a certain size can reconstruct the secret.
>
>Let $s \in \mathbb{F}_p$ be a secret in the [[5.3 Galois Fields|Galois field]] of some prime $p$, and fix integers $n$ and $k \leq n$. The goal is to distribute $n$ shares such that any group of $k$ or more can recover $s$, and any group of fewer than $k$ learns nothing about $s$. To do so:
>1. Construct a random polynomial $P(x) \in \mathbb{F}_{p}[x]$ of degree $k-1$, such that 
>$$P(x) = a_{k-1}x^{k-1} + \cdots + a_1x + s,$$
>where the coefficients $a_{i}$ are chosen uniformly at random from $\mathbb{F}_{p}$.
>2. Distribute the shares $P(1), P(2), \dots, P(n)$ to $n$ participants. Each participant receives one input-output pair $(i, P(i))$.
>
>To recover the secret, any group of $k$ participants combines their $k$ shares $(x_i, y_i)$ to interpolate the unique degree-$(k-1)$ polynomial $P(x)$ using [[5.2 Lagrange Interpolation|Lagrange interpolation]]. The secret is recovered by evaluating $s = P(0)$.
# Intuition
Any group with fewer than $k$ shares has fewer than $k$ points on a degree-$(k-1)$ polynomial, so there are infinitely many such polynomials consistent with those shares. These polynomials can produce any value at $x = 0$, meaning the secret s remains completely undetermined.

# Examples
>[!example] Secrets in the United Nation
>A vault in the United Nations can be opened with a secret combination $s \in \mathbb{Z}$. In only two situations should this vault be opened: 
>1. All 193 member countries must agree, or 
>2. At least 55 countries, plus the U.N. Secretary-General, must agree.
>
>>[!example]- Propose a scheme that gives private information to the Secretary-General and all 193 member countries so that the secret combination $s$ can only be recovered under either one of the two specified conditions.
>>Let $P_{1}(x) = a_{192}x^{192}+\ldots+a_{1}x+s$, and choose $a_{i}$'s at random from $\mathbb{F}_{193}$. Distribute $P_{1}(i)$ where $i=1,\ldots,n$ to the $n$ countries, and distribute $P_{1}(j)$ where $j=n+1, \ldots, n+$ to the Secretary General. 
>
>The General Assembly of the UN decides to add an extra level of security: each of the 193 member countries has a delegation of 12 representatives, all of whom must agree in order for that country to help open the vault.
>>[!example]- Propose a scheme that adds this new feature.
>>
