>[!note] Displacement
>**Displacement** refers to the vector that points from an object’s initial position to its final position, representing the change in position of the object. It can be defined as
>$$\Delta \vec{r} = \vec{r} - \vec{r}_0,$$
>where $\vec{r}_0 \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is the initial $n$-dimensional position of the object at time $t_0$, whereas $\vec{r} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is its final position at another point in time $t$.
>>[!warning] Note!
>>Displacement does not always equal total distance traveled, for example if the person did not walk in a single straight direction.
