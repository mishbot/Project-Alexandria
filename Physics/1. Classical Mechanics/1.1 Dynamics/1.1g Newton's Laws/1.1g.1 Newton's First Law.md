>[!note] Newton's First Law of Motion (Law of Inertia)
>Newton's first law of motion states that an object at rest will remain at rest, and an object in motion will continue moving at a constant velocity in a straight line, *unless acted upon by a net external force*. It is a special case of [[1.1f.2 Newton's Second Law |Newton's second law]].
>>[!warning] Note!
>>Newton's first law only holds in cases of **constant velocity**. Reference frames involving Newton's first law are called inertial reference frames, and are said to be fixed to the Earth. To check if a reference frame is inertial, check if Newton's first law holds.
