>[!note] Discrete-Time Unit Step
>The discrete-time unit step can be defined as a signal $u: \mathbb{Z} \to \mathbb{Z}$, such that
>$$u[n] = \begin{cases}0 & \text{if } n < 0 \\ 1 & \text{if } n \geq 0 \end{cases}.$$
>The unit step can be written in terms of a linear combination of shifted [[2.1a.1 Kronecker Delta| impulses]] as 
>$$u[n] = \sum^{}_{k=0} \delta[n-k] \iff u[n] = \sum^{n}_{l=-\infty}\delta[l] \impliedby l=n-k.$$
