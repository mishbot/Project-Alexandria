>[!note] Vector Norm
>A **vector norm** is a function $\|\cdot\|: \mathcal{X} \to \mathbb{R}$ that provides a way to measure the magnitude of [[3.1. Vector|vectors]] in a vector space. It satisfies the following three properties for all vectors $\vec{x}, \vec{y} \in \mathcal{X}$ and scalars $\alpha \in \mathbb{R}$:
>1. (*Positive-Definiteness*) $\|\vec{x}\| \geq 0$, where $\|\vec{x}\|=0 \iff \vec{x}=\vec{0}$.
>2. (*Triangle Inequality*) $\|\vec{x}+\vec{y}\|\leq\|\vec{x}\|+\|\vec{y}\|$.
>3. (Positive Homogeneity) $\|\alpha \cdot \vec{x}\|=|\alpha|\cdot\|\vec{x}\|$.
