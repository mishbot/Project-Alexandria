>[!note] Transformation Theorem
>The **Transformation Theorem** in probability provides a systematic way to determine the distribution of a transformed random variable subject to some measurable function $g: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R}$. More particularly,
>- If $X$ is a discrete random variable with a [[2.2b.1 Probability Mass Function|probability mass function]] $p_{X}(x)$, then the PMF of $Y=g(X)$ is obtained by summing over all values of $x$ that map to $y$:
>$$p_{Y}(y)=\sum^{}_{\{ x \space \colon \space g(x)=y \}}p_{X}(x),$$
>which simplifies to $p_{Y}(y)=p_{X}(g^{-1}(y))$ if $g$ is invertible/bijective.
