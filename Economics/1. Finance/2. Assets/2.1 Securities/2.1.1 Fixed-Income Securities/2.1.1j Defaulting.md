>[!note] Defaulting
>**Defaulting** occurs when a borrower fails to meet the contractual obligations of a [[2.1.1a Bonds|fixed-income security]]. This typically means the borrower does not make a required payment of interest or principal when due. 
>
>In addition to missing payments, defaulting can also refer to breaching other key terms or covenants of the debt contract, known as a **technical default**. 
>
>To minimize losses in case of default, lenders can buy protection with a [[2.2.5d Credit Default Swap|credit default swap]]. If the borrower defaults, the swap pays $L(1 - R)$ on protected face $L$. Here $R$ is the fraction of face value recovered.
