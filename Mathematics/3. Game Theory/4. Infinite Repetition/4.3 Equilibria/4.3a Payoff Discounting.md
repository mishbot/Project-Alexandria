>[!note] Discounting
>Discounting is the process of reducing the value of payoffs in future stages of an [[4.1 Infinitely Repeated Game|infinitely repeated game]] to account for the preference for immediate rewards over delayed ones. This is represented by a discount factor $\delta \in (0,1)$, applied to future payoffs to compute their present value. A player $i$'s discounted payoff, provided that $i$ plays a strategy $s_i \in S_i$, is then given as
>$$\sum^{}_{i=1}\delta^{i-1}u_i(s_i)=\frac{u_i(s_i)}{1-\delta},$$
>where $u_i$ is the payoff function of player $i$.
