>[!note] Conservative Force
>A conservative force is one which depends only on the initial and final positions of an object, and is independent of the path taken. In Layman terms, it can be a function only of position, and is independent of variables such as time or velocity.
>
>A conservative force may also be thought of as having zero net work done around a closed path.
>
>There are 3 conservative forces: **gravitational**, **electric**, and **elastic**.
