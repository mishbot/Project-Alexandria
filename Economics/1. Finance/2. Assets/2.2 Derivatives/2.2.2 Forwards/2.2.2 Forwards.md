>[!note] Forwards
>A forward contract obliges one party to buy and the other to sell an asset at a fixed price on a fixed future date. No money changes hands when the contract starts. The notes in this folder answer four questions about it:
>- [[2.2.2a Forward Contract]] describes the contract, its long and short payoffs $S_T - K$ and $K - S_T$, how it settles, and its counterparty risk.
>- [[2.2.2b Forward Price]] derives the delivery price that makes a new forward worth zero, $F_0 = S_0e^{rT}$ for a stock with no dividends, with versions for cash dividends and a dividend yield.
>- [[2.2.2c Value of a Forward Contract]] values a contract after it starts, $f = (F_t - K)e^{-r(T-t)}$ for the long.
>- [[2.2.2d Covered Interest Parity]] and [[2.2.2e Forward Rate Agreement]] apply the same argument to exchange rates and to interest rates.
>
>Every result in the folder comes from one argument. A forward can be replicated with the asset and a loan, so any other price allows an arbitrage.

# Prerequisites

- [[2.2.1 Contingent Claim]], for payoffs and replication.
- [[4.2.2a Arbitrage]], for the no-arbitrage argument.
- [[4.2.1. Short Selling]], for the reverse trade.
- [[1.2c Present Value]], for discounting at $e^{-rT}$.

# Contents

- [[2.2.2a Forward Contract]]: contract terms, long and short payoffs, physical and cash settlement, counterparty risk, hedging, and the unequal loss limits of the two sides.
- [[2.2.2b Forward Price]]: the no-arbitrage forward price with no dividends, known cash dividends, a continuous dividend yield, and a yield that changes over time, with the arbitrage trades that enforce it.
- [[2.2.2c Value of a Forward Contract]]: the value of an existing contract for the long and the short in all three income cases, closing a forward early, and the arbitrage when a contract trades at another value.
- [[2.2.2d Covered Interest Parity]]: the forward exchange rate, forward points, premium and discount, the arbitrage in both directions, and the value of a currency forward. **Not taught:** the annual and simple-interest forms; the trading-cost band for currencies; the evidence on deviations since 2008.
- [[2.2.2e Forward Rate Agreement]]: forward rates from zero-coupon bond prices, the FRA payoff and its settlement dates, the fair FRA rate, the value after inception, and hedging a future loan.

# Recommended Path

Read the notes in order. 2.2.2c uses the price formulas of 2.2.2b. 2.2.2d and 2.2.2e can be read in either order. [[2.2.3 Futures]] and [[2.2.4 Cash-and-Carry Arbitrage]] follow this folder, and [[2.2.5a Interest Rate Swap]] builds on 2.2.2e.
