>[!note] Kepler's First Law
>Kepler's first law states that every planet moves in an elliptical orbit, with the Sun (or the central body) located at one of the two foci of the ellipse.

# Intuition
This law states that the path of an orbiting body is not a perfect circle but an ellipse. An ellipse is an elongated circle characterized by two focal points. One of these foci is occupied by the central mass (e.g., the Sun for planets in the solar system). This explains why a planet’s distance from the central body varies throughout its orbit.
