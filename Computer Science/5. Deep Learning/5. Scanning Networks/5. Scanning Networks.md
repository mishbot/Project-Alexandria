>[!note] Scanning Networks
>This section covers networks that apply the same local computation at every position of a structured input. [[5.1. Scanning Networks]] introduces the idea. The [[5.2. Convolutional Neural Networks|convolutional networks]] scan across space, which suits images. The [[5.3. Recurrent Neural Networks|recurrent networks]] scan across time, which suits sequences.

# Prerequisites

- [[2. Neural Networks]], for dense layers and backpropagation.
- [[3. Activation Functions]], for the nonlinearities between layers.

# Contents

- [[5.1. Scanning Networks]]: local computation with shared weights, the idea behind both families.
- [[5.2. Convolutional Neural Networks]]: convolutional layers, pooling, upsampling, CNN architectures and residual networks.
- [[5.3. Recurrent Neural Networks]]: [[5.3.1. RNNs|recurrent neural networks]] for sequences, and [[5.3.2. Backpropagation Through Time|backpropagation through time]] for training them.

# Recommended Path

Start with [[5.2. Convolutional Neural Networks]] and its first note, [[5.2.1. Convolutional Layer]].
