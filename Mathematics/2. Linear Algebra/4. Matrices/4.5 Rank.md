>[!note] Rank
>The **rank** of a matrix is the dimension of its [[4.4a Range|range]] (or equivalently, its row space). It tells us how many linearly independent columns (or rows) exist in the matrix. Formally, for a general matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$,
>$$\operatorname{Rank}(A) \leq \min(m, n),$$
>where if $\operatorname{Rank}(A) = \min(m, n)$, then $A$ is in **full rank**.
>
>Conversely, the **nullity** of a matrix $A$, denoted $\operatorname{Nullity}(A)$ is the dimension of its [[4.4b Null Space|null space]] It represents the number of independent solutions to the homogeneous equation $Ax = 0$. Per the **rank-nullity theorem**, 
>$$\operatorname{Rank}(A) + \operatorname{Nullity}(A) = n.$$

# Properties
#### (*Determinant Relation*) $\operatorname{Rank}(A) = \min(m, n)$ if and only if $\operatorname{\det}(A) \neq 0$.
