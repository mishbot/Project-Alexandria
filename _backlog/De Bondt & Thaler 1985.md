# De Bondt & Thaler (1985) — Long-Term Reversal

**Status:** stub. Forward-linked from [[8.8.1. Cross-Sectional Momentum]], [[8.8.2. Time-Series Momentum]], [[8.8. Momentum]].

The seminal long-term reversal paper. Past three-to-five-year losers outperform past three-to-five-year winners over the subsequent three-to-five years by approximately $25\%$ cumulative. Together with [[8.8.1. Cross-Sectional Momentum|Jegadeesh & Titman]] short-run continuation, defines the joint $1$–$12$ month continuation / $13$–$36$ month reversal pattern that any complete behavioral momentum model must generate.
