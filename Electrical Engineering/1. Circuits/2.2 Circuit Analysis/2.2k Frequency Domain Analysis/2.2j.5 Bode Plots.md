>[!note] Bode Plot
>A **bode plot** are graphical representations of the [[2.2j.4 Transfer Functions|frequency response]] of a system. It consists of two separate plots that display how the system's **magnitude** and **phase** responds to different values of $\omega$ on the logarithmic scale.
>
>The magnitude of a transfer function $H(j\omega)$ in decibels (SI: dB) is given by
>$$20\log_{10}|H(j\omega)|,$$
>and the phase of a transfer function is given by
>$$\phi = \tan^{-1}\frac{\mathrm{Im}\{H(j\omega)\}}{\mathrm{Re}\{H(j\omega)\}}.$$
