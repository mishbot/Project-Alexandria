>[!note] LTI System
>A linear time-invariant (LTI) system $H$ is one that is both:
>1. **Linear**, such that its system response follows the principle of superposition (composed of linearity and additivity):
>	1. Homogeneity: $y(\alpha x)$
>
>1. **Time-invariant**, such that a time shift in its input signal results in a time shift in its output signal:
>$$x(t) \to \boxed{h(n)} \to y(t) \implies x(t-t_0) \to \boxed{h(n)} \to y(t-t_0),$$
>where $t_0 \in \mathbb{R}$.



# Examples

>- $y(n) = x(n)x(n-1)$
