>[!note] Bayesian Network
>A **Bayesian Network (Bayes Net)** is a **probabilistic graphical model** that represents a joint probability distribution over a set of random variables. It captures dependencies among variables using a [[3.1 Graph|directed acyclic graph]], where nodes correspond to variables and edges represent direct probabilistic influence.
>
>Each random variable $X_i$ in the network is associated with a **conditional probability table**, which specifies $P(X_i \mid \text{Parents}(X_i))$. The joint probability distribution over all variables is then given by the **factorization rule for Bayes Nets**:
>$$P(X_1, X_2, \ldots, X_n) = \prod_i P(X_i \mid \text{Parents}(X_i)),$$
>where the factorization follows from the assumption that every node is conditionally independent of all its ancestors given its parents.
