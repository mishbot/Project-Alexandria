>[!note] Periodicity of MCs
>A positive recurrent [[5.1 Markov Chain|Markov chain]] state $x \in \mathcal{X}$ is **periodic** if it has a period equal to the greatest common divisor of all of the time steps $n > 0$ at which the Markov chain, starting from state $x$, has a positive probability of returning to $x$. Formally,
>$$d(x) = \gcd\{ n \in \mathbb{Z}_{+} : P^n(x, x) > 0\}.$$
>If $d(x)=1$, then $x$ is **aperiodic**.
>
>If all of the states in a Markov chain share a common period $d=d(x), \space \forall x \in \mathcal{X}$, then the Markov chain is periodic with period $d$. If $d=1$, then the Markov chain is aperiodic.
