>[!note] Folk Theorem
>The folk theorem states that for an infinitely repeated stage game $G$ with a uniform discount factor $\delta \in (0, 1)$, such that $\delta \to 1$. Then, if all players' payoffs $u_i$ are:
>1. **Feasible**, meaning that they can be achieved arithmetically using some strategy
>2. **Individually rational**, meaning that $u_i > u_i^*$, where $u_i^*$ is player $i$'s worst Nash equilibrium payoff
>
>Then, there exists an SPNE of the repeated game such that each player $i$ receives $u_i$ on average.
