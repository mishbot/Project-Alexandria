>[!note] Static Friction
>**Static friction** is the resistive force that prevents two surfaces in contact from sliding past each other when a force is applied. It acts to oppose the initiation of motion and is present when the objects are stationary relative to each other. Static friction resists any external force up to a certain limit. Mathematically, 
>$$|\vec{F}_{\text{fr}}| \leq \mu_s |\vec{F}_N|,$$
>where $\mu_s$ is the coefficient of static friction.
