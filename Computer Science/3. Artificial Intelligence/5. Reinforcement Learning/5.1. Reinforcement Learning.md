>[!note] Reinforcement Learning
>**Reinforcement Learning (RL)** is a framework for **online planning** where an agent learns to act optimally by interacting with an environment whose transition and reward functions are initially unknown. The agent **explores** by taking actions, observes state transitions and rewards, and gradually learns a **policy** that maximizes cumulative reward.
>
>At each timestep $t$, each tuple $(s_t, a_t, s_{t+1}, r_t)$ is a **sample**, where $s_{t}$ is the **state**, $a_{t}$ is the **action**, and $r_{t}$ is the reward from the taken action). A sequence of samples until termination forms an **episode**.
