>[!note] Work-Energy Principle
>The work-energy principle states that the net work done by all the forces acting on an object is equal to the change in the object’s kinetic energy. Mathematically, this is expressed as
>$$W_\text{net}=\Delta K = K_f - K_i$$
>this implies that if $W_\text{net} < 0,$ the object's kinetic energy has decreased.
