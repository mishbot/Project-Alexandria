>[!note] Newton's Law of Universal Gravitation
>The Law of Universal Gravitation, formulated by Sir Isaac Newton in 1687, states that every point mass in the universe attracts every other point mass with a force that is directly proportional to the product of their masses and inversely proportional to the square of the distance between them. Mathematically, it is expressed as
>$$F_G = G\frac{m_1m_2}{r^2},$$
>where $G=6.67 \times 10^{-11} N \cdot m^2 / kg^2$ is an experimentally derived gravitational constant, and $r$ is the distance between the two masses' cores.
>
>This implies that the force of gravity is inversely proportional to the square of the object's distance from Earth.
