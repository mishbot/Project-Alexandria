>[!note] Perpetuity
>A **perpetuity** is a financial instrument or stream of cash flows that pays a fixed amount of money indefinitely. The payments are typically made at a regular interval of $n$ times per year, and the amount of each payment $C$ remains constant over time. Formally, the [[1.2c Present Value|present value]] of a perpetuity, given an annual percentage rate $r$, is expressed as
>$$\text{PV}=\frac{nC}{r}.$$
>
>Additionally, a **growth perpetuity** is a perpetuity that grows at a constant annual rate of $g$ given a principal $C$:
>$$\text{PV}=\frac{nC}{r-g}.$$
>
>>[!warning] No Initial Payment
>>The standard perpetuity formula assumes that there is no payment at $t_{0}$. The formula for a ***perpetuity-due*** is given as 
>>$$\text{PV}=\frac{C(n+r-g)}{r-g}.$$
