>[!note] Recurrent Neural Networks
>This section covers networks that read a sequence one step at a time and carry a hidden state forward. A CNN shares one kernel across positions in space. An RNN shares one update rule across steps in time. [[5.3.1. RNNs]] starts from filters in signal processing. An FIR filter is a convolution over time, and an IIR filter keeps a state. The linear state-space model is the simplest IIR filter, and the RNN adds a nonlinearity to its update. The note also covers stacked RNNs and the Kalman filter as a learned linear RNN. [[5.3.2. Backpropagation Through Time]] trains an RNN by unrolling it over the sequence. It explains vanishing and exploding gradients and the cost of the sequential steps. [[5.3.3. Sequence-to-Sequence Models]] pairs an encoder RNN with a decoder RNN for tasks such as translation. Its bottleneck leads to attention.
