>[!note] Exotic Options
>An **exotic option** is a contract whose payoff rule differs from a standard European or American call or put. Dealers design them to match an exposure or an investment view more closely than a vanilla option can. Most are priced with the same tools as vanilla options: the [[2.3.3e Black-Scholes-Merton Equation|BSM equation]], risk-neutral expectations, and replication. The section has three folders, one per category, with one note per concept.
>- [[2.3.6.1 Perpetual and Binary Options]] holds options that never expire and options that pay a fixed amount or nothing. [[2.3.6.1a Perpetual American Options]] solve the BSM equation without its time term, and smooth pasting sets the exercise level $S^*$. A cash [[2.3.6.1b Binary Options|binary call]] is worth $He^{-r\tau}N(d_2)$ and is the limit of a narrow call spread.
>- [[2.3.6.2 Path-Dependent Options]] holds options whose payoffs use prices observed before expiry. [[2.3.6.2a Barrier Options]] satisfy in-out parity, $C_{KO} + C_{KI} = C$. [[2.3.6.2b Asian Options]] pay on an average, and [[2.3.6.2c Lookback Options]] pay on the highest or lowest price.
>- [[2.3.6.3 Basket Options and Structured Products]] holds options on several assets and the notes built from options: [[2.3.6.3a Basket Options]], [[2.3.6.3b Autocallables]], [[2.3.6.3c Cliquets]], [[2.3.6.3d Principal-Protected Notes]] and [[2.3.6.3e Reverse Convertible Notes]].
>
