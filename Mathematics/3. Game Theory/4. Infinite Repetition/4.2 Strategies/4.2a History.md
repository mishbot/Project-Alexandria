>[!note] History
>The history $h_t$ of a [[4.1 Infinitely Repeated Game|repeated game]] at period $t$ is the set of all possible actions taken by all players at times $\tau < t$. Formally, the history of a repeated game at time $t$ given the strategy profile of every stage game at $\tau < t$ can be expressed as
>$$h_t = s_2 \times s_3 \times \ldots \times s_{t-1}$$
>>[!warning] Note!
>>The history of period $1$ is simply $h_1=\emptyset$, since there is nothing occurring before stage $1$ of the game.
# Properties
##### At period $t \geq 2$, the set $h_t$  specifies $n^{t-1}$ actions.

# Example
>[!example] Infinitely Repeated Prisoner's Dilemma
>Consider again the infinitely repeated Prisoner's Dilemma of two players. At $t=1$, there is no history, so $h_1=\emptyset$. However, at $t=1$, players can play the strategies $s_i=\{D, C\}$. As such, the history at $t=2$ is $h_2=\{(D, D), (D, C), (C, D), (C, C)\}$. Then, at $t=3$, players can again play the strategies $s_i\{D, C\}$. This leads to 
>$$h_3=\{\{(D, D), (D, D)\}, \{(D, D), (D, C)\}, \ldots, \{(C, C), (C, D)\}, \{(C, C), (C, C)\}\},$$
>specifying a complete history of all possible paths of play up to $t-1$.
