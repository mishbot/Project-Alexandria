>[!note] Scattering
>**Scattering** refers to the interaction of waves with a boundary or medium, resulting in portions of the wave being reflected, transmitted, or absorbed. There are two types of general scattering:
>1. **Reflection** occurs when a wave hits a new medium (such as a fixed support), and by Newton's third law, the support invokes an equal and opposite in magnitude reaction, thereby causing the wave to invert and proceed to travel in the opposite direction.
>2. Transmission occurs when a wave passes through a medium or boundary and continues to propagate in the new medium.
