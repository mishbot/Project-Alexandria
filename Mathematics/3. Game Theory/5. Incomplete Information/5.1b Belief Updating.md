>[!note] Belief Updating
> Belief updating in [[5.1  Bayesian Game|Bayesian games]] involves players revising their beliefs about other players’ types as the game progresses and new information becomes available. If a player $i \in N$ wants to know the probability of player $j \in N$ playing some type $t_j \in T_j$, then
> - The **prior distribution** over the types of player $j$ from the perspective of $i$ is a probability distribution $P_j: t_j \to [0, 1]$ that represents $i$’s initial beliefs about the types of $j$ before any actions are observed or any information is revealed.
> - The **[[1.4b Bayes' Rule|posterior distribution]]** over the types of player $j$ from the perspective of $i$ is a probability distribution $P_j(\cdot|t_i): t_j \to [0,1]$ that represents $i$’s updated beliefs about $j$'s after observing actions or receiving information.

# Properties
#### *(Independence)* If $P_j(t_j | t_i) = P_j(t_j)$, then $t_i$ gives no information about $t_j$.
