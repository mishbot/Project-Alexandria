>[!note] Lagrangian Duality
>A **Lagrangian dual** is a type of duality for primal optimization problems problems of the form
>$$\begin{align*} p^* = \min\limits_{x \in X} \quad & f_{0}(x) \\ \text{s.t.:} \quad & f_{i}(x) \le 0, \quad i=1,\ldots,m, \\ \quad & h_{j}(x) = 0, \quad j = 1, \ldots, k, \end{align*}$$
>such that the constraints are embedded into the objective via the multipliers $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}^m$ and $\mu \in \mathbb{R}^k$: 
>$$\mathcal{L}(x,\lambda,\mu)=f_{0}(x)+\sum^{m}_{i=1}\lambda_{i}f_{i}(x)+\sum^{k}_{j=1}\mu_{i}h_{i}(x).$$
>Then, the Lagrangian dual problem is given as
>$$d^*=\sup_{\lambda \geq 0}\inf_{x \in X}\mathcal{L}(x,\lambda,\mu).$$
