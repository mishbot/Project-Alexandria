>[!note] Power
>Power (SI: Watt / $\text{W} \equiv \text{J} / \text{s}$) quantifies how rapidly energy is transferred or transformed. It measures the rate at which a system does work or converts energy, providing insight into how fast a physical process occurs.
>
>More particularly, **average power** denotes the total amount of energy transferred, divided by the total time it takes, such that
>$$\bar{P} = \frac{E}{\Delta t} = \frac{W}{\Delta t},$$
>where $W$ is the net work done and $E$ is the energy transferred over some time frame $\Delta t = t_{f}-t_{i}$.
>
>Similarly, **instantaneous power** describes the rate of energy transfer at a specific infinitesimal point in time, such that
>$$P = \frac{dE}{dt} = \vec{F} \cdot \vec{v}.$$
