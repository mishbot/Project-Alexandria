>[!note] Compounding
>**Compounding** is the process by which [[1.1 Fisher's Equation|interest]] earned (or charged) on a principal $P$ also begins to earn additional interest in subsequent periods. 
>
>Cash flows can be compounded using the nominal interest rate over one compounding period per year, also known as the **Annual Percentage Rate (APR)**, or using its geometric average, the **Effective Annual Rate (EAR)**, over $n$ compounding periods per year, such that:
>$$\operatorname{EAR} = \left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^n-1.$$
>
>Letting $n \to \infty$ gives continuous compounding:
>$$\lim_{ n \to \infty }\left[\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^n-1\right] = e^r-1,$$
>for which $\ln(1 + \text{EAR})=r$.
