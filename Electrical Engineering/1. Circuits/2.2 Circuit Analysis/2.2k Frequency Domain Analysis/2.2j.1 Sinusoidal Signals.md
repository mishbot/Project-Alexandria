>[!note] Sinusoidal Signals
>A **sinusoidal signal** is a type of continuous wave that oscillates smoothly and periodically over time, following the mathematical form of a sine or cosine function. Sinusoidal signals are the core of AC analysis. A sinusoidal signal can be represented of the form
>$$x(t) = A\cos(\omega t + \phi),$$
>where $A$ is the signal's magnitude, $\omega=2\pi f = \frac{2\pi}{T}$ is its angular frequency, and $\phi$ is its phase angle (the amount by which it is shifted from the standard cosine waveform).
