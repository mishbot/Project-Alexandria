> [!note] Screw (Helical) Joint
> A **screw (helical) joint** is a one-DOF joint that couples rotation about a fixed axis with translation along the same axis.
> 
> We choose a unit axis direction $\omega$ (right-hand rule) and pick any point $q$ on that axis. We also define the pitch $h$ as translation per radian of rotation (units: length/rad).
> 
> Because the point undergoes rotation about the axis exactly like a revolute joint, but also drifts along the axis as it rotates, the instantaneous point velocity is:
>$$\dot p(t)=\dot\theta(t)\Big(\omega\times\bigl(p(t)-q\bigr)+h\,\omega\Big).$$
>
> Rotating about an axis through q contributes the same “moment arm” term as the revolute joint, while the additional translation along the axis contributes $h\omega$. Therefore the linear part of the twist must satisfy:
>$$v=-\omega\times q+h\,\omega, \qquad \omega^T v=h \quad (\|\omega\|=1).$$
>
> A twist stacks the linear and angular velocity components:
>$$\xi=\begin{bmatrix}v\\ \omega\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}-\omega\times q+h\,\omega\\ \omega\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> Because $\hat{\omega}$ is the matrix that implements cross products (i.e., $\hat{\omega}x=\omega\times x$), the twist in $\mathfrak{se}(3)$ is:
>$$\hat{\xi}= \begin{bmatrix}\hat{\omega} & v\\ 0&0\end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}\hat{\omega} & -\hat{\omega}q+h\,\omega\\ 0&0\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> Then, $\hat{\xi}$ is the infinitesimal generator of the motion in homogeneous coordinates. The instantaneous and finite kinematics satisfy:
>$$\begin{bmatrix}\dot p(\theta)\\ 0\end{bmatrix}=\hat{\xi}\begin{bmatrix}p(\theta)\\ 1\end{bmatrix},\qquad \begin{bmatrix}p(\theta)\\ 1\end{bmatrix}=e^{\hat{\xi}\theta}\begin{bmatrix}p(0)\\ 1\end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> Exponentiating the generator yields a rotation $e^{\hat{\omega}\theta}$ about the axis, plus a translation of magnitude $h\theta$ along $\omega$, plus the same induced translation term from rotating about an axis through $q$. Therefore, the transform is:
>$$e^{\hat{\xi}\theta}= \begin{bmatrix} e^{\hat{\omega}\theta} & \bigl(I-e^{\hat{\omega}\theta}\bigr)q+h\,\omega\,\theta\\ 0&1 \end{bmatrix}.$$
>
> If $h=0$, the screw joint reduces to a revolute joint; if $\omega=0$, it reduces to a prismatic joint (pure translation).
