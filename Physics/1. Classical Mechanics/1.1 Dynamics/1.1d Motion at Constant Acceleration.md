>[!note] Motion at Constant Acceleration
>For a time frame $[t_0, t_f]$ where [[1.1c.2 Instantaneous Acceleration|acceleration]] remains constant at $\vec{a}$, the following formulae are derived from $a = \frac{v - v_0}{t}$:
>$$
>\begin{align}
>v &= v_0 + at && \enspace \text{(Velocity)} \\
>x &= x_0+v_0t+\frac{1}{2}at^2 && \enspace \text{(Displacement)} \\
>v^2 &= v_0^2+2a(x-x_0) && \enspace \text{(Velocity-Displacement)}
>\end{align}
>$$
