>[!note] Refrigerators
>A refrigerator is a heat engine run backward. Work $W$ moves heat $Q_L$ out of a cold space and dumps $Q_L + W$ into a hot one. The [[2.7.5a Refrigerator|refrigerator]] note measures the machine by $\text{COP} = Q_L/W$ and gives the Carnot limit $T_L/(T_H - T_L)$. The [[2.7.5b Heat Pump|heat pump]] is the same machine used for heating, measured by $\text{COP}_{\text{hp}} = Q_H/W$.

# Prerequisites

- [[2.7.4b Carnot Engine]], for the cycle that a refrigerator runs in reverse.
- [[2.7.1 Heat Engine]], for the reservoir diagram and the signs of $Q_H$ and $Q_L$.

# Contents

- [[2.7.5b Heat Pump]]: the same machine used for heating, with $\text{COP}_{\text{hp}} = \text{COP}_{\text{ref}} + 1$. **Not taught:** the fluid, coils, and reversing valve.
