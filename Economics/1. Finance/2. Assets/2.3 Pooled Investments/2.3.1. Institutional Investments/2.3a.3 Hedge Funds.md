>[!note] Hedge Funds
>**Hedge funds** are pooled investment vehicles that employ a wide range of strategies–such as long/short equity, global macro, event-driven, and arbitrage–primarily in public markets.
>
>They aim to generate high absolute returns regardless of market direction, often using debt financing (leverage), derivatives, and short selling to amplify gains and hedge risks. 
>
>Managed by professional investment advisors, hedge funds typically cater to institutional investors and high-net-worth individuals, operating with fewer regulatory constraints than mutual funds.
