>[!note] Defaulting
>**Defaulting** occurs when a borrower fails to meet the contractual obligations of a [[2.1a.1 Bonds|fixed-income security]]. This typically means the borrower does not make a required payment of interest or principal when due. 
>
>In addition to missing payments, defaulting can also refer to breaching other key terms or covenants of the debt contract, known as a **technical default**. 
>
>To minimize losses in case of default, lenders Credit default swaps are contracts
