>[!note] Private Equity
>**Private equity firms** are investment management companies that raise capital from institutional and high-net-worth investors to acquire controlling ownership stakes in established but failing privately held or publicly traded companies. 
>
>The goal of PE is to increase the value of these businesses through operational improvements, strategic guidance, and financial restructuring, often using significant leverage (debt financing), before exiting through a sale or IPO.
>
>As with VCs and hedge funds, private equity firms earn profit based on the **2 and 20 model**, which utilizes a 2% management fee (based on AUM), plus 20% of all profits.
