>[!note] Instantaneous Acceleration
>The **instantaneous acceleration**, $a$, of an object is defined as the limiting value of the [[1.1c.1 Average Acceleration|average acceleration]] as we let $\Delta t$ approach zero, such that
>$$\vec{a} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0}\frac{\Delta \vec{v}}{\Delta t} = \frac{d \vec{v}}{dt}.$$
