# Multi-View Reconstruction

Multi-view reconstruction is the pipeline that recovers both the **3D structure** of a scene and the **poses** of the cameras that observed it, starting from a collection of $n \geq 2$ images. It extends [[5.3. Epipolar Geometry|two-view geometry]] from a single pair to many views and connects to [[4. Feature-Based Alignment|feature-based alignment]] as its front end.

The pipeline is classically split in two:

1. **[[6.1. Structure from Motion (SfM)|Structure from Motion]]** — recovers camera poses $(R_i, T_i)$ and a **sparse** 3D point cloud (one point per matched feature) by combining the [[5.3. Epipolar Geometry|essential matrix]], [[5.2. Stereo Geometry|triangulation]], Perspective-n-Point, and bundle adjustment.
2. **[[6.2. Multi-View Stereo (MVS)|Multi-View Stereo]]** — given the SfM-recovered poses, reconstructs a **dense** surface by solving a per-pixel depth search across multiple views, then fusing the depth maps into a mesh.

### Notes
- [[6.1. Structure from Motion (SfM)]] — incremental pipeline for sparse reconstruction
- [[6.2. Multi-View Stereo (MVS)]] — dense reconstruction following SfM
