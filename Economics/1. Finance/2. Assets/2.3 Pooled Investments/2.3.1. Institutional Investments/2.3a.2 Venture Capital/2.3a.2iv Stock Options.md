>[!note] Stock Options
>**Employee Stock Options** are a form of equity compensation that give employees the right–but not the obligation–to purchase a specified number of company shares at a fixed price (called the **strike price**) after meeting certain conditions, such as vesting. 
>
>The **strike price** is usually set at the Fair Market Value (FMV) of the company at the time the option is granted, as determined by a third-party valuation firm (e.g., through a 409A valuation in the U.S.).
>
>ESOs are typically given with a vesting schedule–typically 4 years with a **1-year cliff** (meaning the first 25% vests after one year, and the rest vests monthly or quarterly thereafter). They can be exercised after they vest and within a **fixed post-termination window** (often 90 days after leaving the company).
