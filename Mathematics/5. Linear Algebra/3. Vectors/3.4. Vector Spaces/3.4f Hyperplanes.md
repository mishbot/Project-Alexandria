>[!note] Hyperplane
>A hyperplane in an $n$-dimensional vector space $\mathcal{X}^n$ is a flat, affine subspace of dimension $n - 1$ that divides  $\mathcal{X}^n$ into two half-spaces; one below the hyperplane $\mathcal{H}_{-}$ and one above the hyperplane $\mathcal{H}_{++}$. In $n$-dimensional Euclidean space, It is defined as the set of all points $\vec{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ that satisfy a linear equation:
>$$\mathcal{H} = \{ x \in \mathbb{R} : a^Tx = b \},$$
>where $a \in \mathbb{R}^n$ ($\vec{a} \neq 0$) and $b \in \mathbb{R}$ are given.
