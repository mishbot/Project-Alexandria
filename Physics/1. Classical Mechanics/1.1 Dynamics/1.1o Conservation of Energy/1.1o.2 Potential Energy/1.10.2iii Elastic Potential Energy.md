>[!note] Elastic Potential Energy
>Elastic potential energy is energy stored in elastic materials as a result of their stretching or compression, such as in a spring. It is given by
>$$U_S = \frac{1}{2}kx,$$
>where $x$ is the displacement of the elastic object, such that $x=0$ is its displacement at its equilibrium (i.e. its resting position).
