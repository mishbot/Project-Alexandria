>[!note] Noninverting Amplifier
>A noninverting amplifier is an [[2.1h.1 Ideal Op-Amp|op-amp]] configuration that does not invert the source voltage and simply adds a gain to the output voltage, such that $V_{o}=GV_{s}$, where $G=\frac{R_{1}+R_{2}}{R_{2}}$. The figure below visually describes the configuration.
