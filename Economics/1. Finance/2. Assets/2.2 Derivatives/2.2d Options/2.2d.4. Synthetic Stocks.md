>[!note] Synthetic Stock
>A **synthetic stock** is a portfolio that replicates holding one share of the underlying by combining options with a risk-free position. 
>
>Using European options with the same strike $K$ and maturity $T$, put–call parity implies
>$$C - P = S - Ke^{-rT} \quad\Rightarrow\quad S = C - P + Ke^{-rT}.$$
>So a **synthetic long stock** is **long a call**, **short a put**, and **lend** $Ke^{-rT}$ today so the deposit grows to $K$ at $T$. By the law of one price, this synthetic position should be priced the same as the actual stock (up to implementation frictions).
>
>More generally, put–call parity lets you “solve” for other synthetics by rearranging the same identity, so you can replicate a call, a put, or a risk-free loan using the other instruments.
