>[!note] Potential Energy
>Potential energy is the energy stored in an object or system due to its position, arrangement, or state. It represents the potential for an object to perform work as a result of its position in a force field or its configuration relative to other objects. Potential energy is exclusively associated with [[1.1o.1 Conservative Forces|conservative forces]]. We can define the change in potential energy associated with a particular conservative force as the negative of the work done by that force, such that
>$$\Delta U = -\int^{2}_{1}\vec{F}dx + C=-W.$$
>when an object moves from position 1 to position 2.
>
>Similarly, force can be expressed as the derivative of potential energy, such that
>$$F(x)=-\frac{d}{dx}U(x)$$
>>[!warning] Note!
>>Since an integral depending only on position is exclusive to conservative forces, potential energy can only be expressed for such forces.
