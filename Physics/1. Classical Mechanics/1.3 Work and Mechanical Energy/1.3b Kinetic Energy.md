>[!note] Kinetic Energy
>**Kinetic energy** (SI: Joule / $\text{J} \equiv \text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2 \cdot \text{s}^{-2}$) quantifies the motion of an object in terms of how difficult it would be to stop it. The faster and more massive an object, the harder it is to stop, and the more kinetic energy it has.
>
>Mathematically, the kinetic energy of an object of mass m moving with speed v is given by the equation
>$$K = \frac{1}{2}mv^2.$$
