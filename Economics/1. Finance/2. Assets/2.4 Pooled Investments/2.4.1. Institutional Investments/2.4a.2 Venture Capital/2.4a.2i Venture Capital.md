>[!note] Venture Capital
>**Venture capital firms** provide funding to early-stage, high-growth potential startups in exchange for equity ownership. Unlike private equity firms, they typically do not utilize debt financing (leverage), do not hold a majority stake in their investments, and instead take on higher risk in pursuit of outsized returns. 
>
>The goal of VCs is to help startups scale rapidly and eventually exit through an acquisition or IPO, realizing significant gains on their equity stake.
