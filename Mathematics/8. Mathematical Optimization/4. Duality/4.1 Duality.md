>[!note] Duality
>**Duality** in optimization is the framework that associates every optimization problem, known as the **primal problem**, with another similar problem on the dual space called the **dual problem**. Let $F: X \times Y \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty \}$ be a **perturbation function** (i.e., $F(x, 0) = f(x)$), and $p^*$ be the solution to the primal problem
>$$p^* = \inf_{x \in X} f(x).$$
>Then, $p^*$ can be associated with an **always convex** dual optimization problem on the dual space $Y^*$, such that
>$$d^* = \sup_{y^* \in Y^*} \{ -F^*(0, y^*) \}.$$
>The values between these problems are always related via the **weak duality principle**, such that $$p^* \geq d^*.$$
>Moreover, **strong duality** holds (i.e., $p^* = d^*$) if certain constraint qualifications are met.
