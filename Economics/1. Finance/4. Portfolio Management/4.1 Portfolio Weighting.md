>[!note] Portfolio Weighting
>Portfolio weighting is the process of determining what percentage of the total portfolio value $V$ that each asset should represent, such that each asset's value is equal to $V_{i}=w_{i}V$. A portfolio of $n$ assets can be
>- **Equal-weighted**: all assets have a uniform weight of $w_{i}=\frac{1}{n}$.
>- **Price-weighted**: the $i$-th asset has a weight of $w_{i}=\dfrac{P_{i}}{\sum^{n}_{i=1}P_{i}}$ for a share price $P_{i}$.
>- **Value-weighted (cap-weighted)**: the $i$-th asset has a weight of $w_{i}=\dfrac{P_{i} \cdot S_{i}}{\sum^{n}_{i=1}P_{i} \cdot S_{i}}$ for a share price $P_{i}$ and $S_{i}$ total outstanding shares.
