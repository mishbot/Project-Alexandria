>[!note] Epigraph
>The **epigraph** of a function $f: X \to \bar{\mathbb{R}}$ is the set of points on or above $f$. Formally, 
>$$\operatorname{epi} f = \{ (x,t) \in X \times \mathbb{R} : f(x) \leq t \}.$$
>Similarly, the **interior** of $f$ can be defined as the strict (open) epigraph (i.e., the points in the range of $f$ are excluded from the set).
