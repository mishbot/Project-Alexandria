>[!note] Range
>Given a matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, the **range** (also referred to as the column space) of $A$ is a [[3.4a Vector (Sub)spaces|vector subspace]] in $\mathbb{R}^m$ the represents the [[3.4b Span|span]] of its columns, defined as
>$$\mathcal{R}(A)=\operatorname{Span}(\vec{a}_1, \vec{a}_2, …, \vec{a}_n)=\left\{\sum^{n}_{i=1}\alpha_i \vec{a}_i \text{ | } \alpha_i \in \mathbb{R}\right\}\subseteq \mathbb{R}^m,$$
>where $\alpha_{i}$ is the scaling factor (or weight) of the $i$-th column vector of $A$. The range of $A$ can also be represented as $\operatorname{Col}(A)$.
>
>Similarly, the **row space** of $A$, denoted $\operatorname{Row}(A) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n$, is the span of its rows.
>
>>[!warning] Spaces of $A$ and $A^T$
>>It is important to note that $\operatorname{Col}(A)=\operatorname{Row}(A^T)$ and vice-versa.
