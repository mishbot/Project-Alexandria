>[!note] Satellite Motion
>The orbital speed of a satellite defines the minimum speed at which an object must travel to maintain a stable, circular orbit around a central body under the influence of gravitational force. Mathematically, the velocity of a satellite around an attractor can be defined as
>$$v_\text{orb} = \sqrt{\frac{GM}{r}},$$
>where $M$ is the mass of the attractor and $r$ is its radius.
