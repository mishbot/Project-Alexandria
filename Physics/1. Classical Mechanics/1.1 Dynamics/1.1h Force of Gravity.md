>[!note] Force of Gravity (Weight)
>The force of gravity of an object can be expressed as
>$$\vec{F}_G = mg,$$
>where $m$ is the mass of the object. The magnitude $|\vec{F}_G|$ of the gravitational force applied on an object is known as its **weight**.
>
>When an object is at rest on a surface, the force of gravity continues to act on it; however, the normal (perpendicular) force, expressed as $\vec{F}_N$, equally opposes $\vec{F}_G$, causing the object to remain at rest as the net force applied on it is zero:
>```tikz
>\begin{document}
>
>\begin{tikzpicture}
>
>    % Draw the table
>    \draw[thick] (-2,0) -- (2,0); % table surface
>
>    % Draw the box
>    \draw[fill=gray] (-0.5,0) rectangle (0.5,1);
>
>    % Label the box
>    \node at (0, 0.5) {m};
>
>    % Draw the force of gravity (downward)
>    \draw[->, thick, magenta] (0,0) -- (0,-1) node[below] {$\vec{F}_G$};
>
>    % Draw the normal force (upward)
>    \draw[->, thick, magenta] (0,1) -- (0,2) node[above] {$\vec{F}_N$};
>
>\end{tikzpicture}
>
>\end{document}
>```
