>[!note] p-Norm
>For a vector $\vec{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$, the $\ell^p$  [[3.5a Vector Norm|norm]] is expressed as
>$$||\vec{x}||_{p}=\left(\sum^{n}_{i=1}|x_{i}|^p\right)^{\frac{1}{p}}.$$
>Special cases of the $p$-norm include $p=1$ (the Manhattan norm), $p=2$ (the Euclidean norm), and $p=\infty$ (the Chebyshev norm), such that $||\vec{x}||_{\infty}=\max(\vec{x})$ is the largest element of $\vec{x}$.
>
>Then, the $\ell^{p}$ **unit norm ball** is defined as the set of all vectors with $\ell^p$ less than or equal to one,
>$$\mathcal{B}^p = \{ \vec{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n : \|\vec{x}\|_{p} \leq 1 \},$$
