>[!note] Annuity
>An **annuity** is a financial instrument or series of cash flows that pays a fixed amount of money at $n$ intervals per year for $t$ years, with each payment $C$ remaining constant over the term. The payments can be made at the end of each period (ordinary annuity) or at the beginning (annuity due). Formally, the present value of an ordinary annuity, given an annual percentage rate $r$, is expressed as:
>$$PV=C \times \left[\frac{1}{r}-\frac{1}{r(1+r)^{nt}}\right].$$
>
>Additionally, a **growth perpetuity** is a perpetuity that grows at a constant rate of $g$ per year given a principal $C$:
>$$PV=C \times \frac{1}{r-g} \left[1-\left(\frac{1+g}{1+r}\right)^{nt}\right].$$
