>[!note] Corporate Restructuring
>Corporate restructuring refers to transactions that change a firm’s ownership structure, asset boundaries, or capital allocation, often by separating business units into distinct entities.
>- A **carve-out** is when a parent firm sells a **minority equity stake** in a subsidiary to outside investors, typically through an IPO, while the parent usually retains control and a majority ownership stake.
>- A **spin-off** is when a parent firm **distributes shares** of a subsidiary it owns to the parent’s ***existing shareholders***, creating a separately traded company and reducing or eliminating the parent’s ownership stake without selling the shares for cash.
