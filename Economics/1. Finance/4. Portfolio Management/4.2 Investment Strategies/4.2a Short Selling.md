>[!note] Short Selling
>**Short selling** is an investment strategy where an investor borrows shares of a security, sells them immediately at the current market price, and then later buys back the same number of shares to return to the lender and profit from the decline in share price.
>
>Short calls have a predetermined expiration date specified in the option contract, after which the option becomes void and the lender retains the net return with no further obligations.
