>[!note] Independence
>Events $X_{1}, \ldots, X_{n}$ in the same [[1.1 Probability Space|probability space]] are said to be **pairwise independent** if for all $i \neq j$ ,
>$$\mathbb{P}[X_{i} \cap X_{j}] = \mathbb{P}(X_{i}) \cdot \mathbb{P}(X_{j}).$$
>
>Furthermore, the events are **mutually independent** if
>$$\mathbb{P}\left( \bigcap\limits_{i=1}^{n} X_{i} \right) = \prod^{n}_{i=1}\mathbb{P}(X_{i}).$$
