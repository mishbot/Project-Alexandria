>[!note] Average Velocity
>The **average velocity** of an object is a vector quantity defined as the [[1.1a Displacement|displacement]], $\Delta \vec{r}$, divided by the elapsed time $\Delta t$,  such that
>$$\vec{v}_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\vec{r} - \vec{r}_0}{t - t_0} = \frac{\Delta \vec{r}}{\Delta t}.$$

# Properties
#### *(Midpoint)* Average velocity is equal to the midpoint of the initial and final velocities, s. t.  $\bar{v} = \frac{v + v_0}{2}$.
