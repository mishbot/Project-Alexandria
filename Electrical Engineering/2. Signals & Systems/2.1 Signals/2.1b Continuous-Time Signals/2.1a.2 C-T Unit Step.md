>[!note] Continuous-Time Unit Step
>The continuous-time unit step can be defined as a signal $u$, such that
>$$u(t) = \begin{cases}0 & \text{if } t < 0 \\ 1 & \text{if } t \geq 0 \end{cases}.$$
>The unit step can be written as the integral of the [[2.1a.1 Dirac Delta|Dirac delta]], such that
>$$u(t) = \int^{\infty}_{-\infty}\delta(\tau)d\tau.$$
