>[!note] Inverting Amplifier
>An inverting amplifier is an [[2.1h.1 Ideal Op-Amp|op-amp]] configuration that utilizes [[2.1h.2 Negative Feedback|negative feedback]] to invert the source voltage and adds a gain to the output voltage, such that $V_{o}=GV_{s}$, where $G=-\frac{R_{f}}{R_{i}}$.
