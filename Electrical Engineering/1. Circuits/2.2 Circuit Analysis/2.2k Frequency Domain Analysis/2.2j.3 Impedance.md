>[!note] Impedance
>The impedance $Z$ (SI: Ohm/$\Omega$) of any circuit element is defined as the ratio of the [[2.2j.2 Phasor|phasor]] voltage across it to the phasor current entering through its plus (+) terminal, such that
>$$Z = \frac{V}{I}.$$

# Proofs
#### (Resistor Impedance) $Z_R = R$

#### (Capacitor Impedance) $Z_C = \frac{1}{j\omega C} = -\frac{j}{\omega C}$

#### (Inductor Impedance) $Z_L = j\omega L$
