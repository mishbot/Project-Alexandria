>[!note] Second-Order Circuits
>Second-order circuits are electrical circuits whose behavior is described by a second-order differential equation. These circuits contain both [[2.1i Inductor|inductors]] and [[2.1g Capacitor|capacitors]]. Second-order circuits follow the general equation
>$$\frac{d^2}{dt^2}x(t) + 2\alpha\frac{d}{dt}x(t) + \omega_0^2x(t) = f(t),$$
>where $\alpha$ is the damping coefficient, and $\omega_0$ is the resonant frequency of the circuit at which the impedance is purely real.
>
