>[!note] Gravitational Potential Energy
>Gravitational potential energy (GPE) is the energy that an object possesses due to its position in a gravitational field. It is associated with the potential work that can be done by gravity as the object moves within the field. Mathematically, gravitational potential energy for an object near the Earth’s surface is defined by
>$$U_G = mgy,$$
>where $y$ is the object's position relative to the lowest point in the system (usually the ground).

# Intuition
Take any object. Lift it up by a displacement $d$. You have now done work on that object equal to $mgd$. Once you've lifted the object to the desired height, the object also has gravitational potential energy equal to $mgd$. This is because you are the only thing stopping it from hitting back the ground.

This can also be said for another reference point: Earth's core. When an object is on the ground, it has gravitational potential of $mgh$, where $h$ is the distance between Earth's surface and Earth's core.

Potential energy is only dependent on the current of the object relative to some reference point.
