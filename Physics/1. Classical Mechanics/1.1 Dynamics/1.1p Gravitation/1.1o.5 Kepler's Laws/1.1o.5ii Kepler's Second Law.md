>[!note] Kepler's Second Law
>Kepler's second law states that an imaginary line segment joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas during equal intervals of time.

# Intuition
This law indicates that a planet moves faster when it is closer to the Sun and slower when it is farther from the Sun. The result is that the area swept out by the line segment (or radius vector) connecting the planet to the Sun is constant over equal periods. This law reflects the conservation of angular momentum and explains why orbits accelerate as the body moves closer to the central mass.
