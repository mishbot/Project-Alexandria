> [!note] Rejection Sampling
> **Rejection Sampling** is an approximate inference method for Bayes Nets that improves upon [[7.4.1. Prior Sampling|prior sampling]] by **discarding samples inconsistent with the evidence as early as possible**, reducing wasted computation while preserving correctness. For a sample to be valid, parents must be sampled before their children.
> 
> Sampling follows the same procedure as prior sampling, but with an added rule:
>- If at any point a sampled variable contradicts the evidence, the sample is **rejected immediately**.    
>
> The posterior is estimated as:
> $$P(Q \mid E) \approx \frac{\#\{\text{accepted samples with } Q=q \text{ and } E=e\}}{\#\{\text{accepted samples with } E=e\}}.$$
> 
> This works because every accepted sample still occurs with probability proportional to the true joint distribution.
> 
> **Limitation:** when the evidence has very low probability, nearly all samples are rejected. Although rejection sampling is more efficient than prior sampling, it still performs poorly under unlikely evidence.
