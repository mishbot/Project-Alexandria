>[!note] Dot Product of Two Vectors
>The dot product of two [[3.3 Vector Multiplication|vectors]] $\vec{x},\vec{y} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is given as
>$$
>\vec{x} \cdot \vec{y}=\vec{y}^T \vec{x}
>=\begin{bmatrix}y_1 & y_2 & \dots & y_n\end{bmatrix}
>\begin{bmatrix}x_1 \\ x_2 \\ \vdots \\ x_n\end{bmatrix}
>=x_1y_1+x_2y_2+\dots+x_ny_n=\sum^{n}_{i=1}x_iy_i.
>$$
