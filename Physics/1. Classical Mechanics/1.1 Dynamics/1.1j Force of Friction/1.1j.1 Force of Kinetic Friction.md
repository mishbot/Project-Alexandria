>[!note] Kinetic Friction
>**Kinetic friction** (also known as **sliding friction**) is the resistive force that acts between two surfaces in contact when they are moving relative to each other. The magnitude force of friction $\vec{F}_{\text{fr}}$ is directly proportional to the magnitude of the normal force $\vec{F}_N$, such that
>$$|\vec{F}_{\text{fr}}|=\mu_k |\vec{F}_N|,$$
>where $\mu_k$ is the coefficient of kinetic friction, and depends of the roughness of the two surfaces.
