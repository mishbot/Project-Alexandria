>[!note] Complex Conjugate
>The complex conjugate of a complex number is formed by changing the sign of its imaginary part. For a complex number $z = a + bj = Ae^{j\theta}$ , where $a$ and $b$ are real numbers and $i$ is the imaginary unit (with  $i^2 = -1$), the complex conjugate of $z$ is denoted by  $z\textasteriskcentered$  and is defined as:
>$$z\textasteriskcentered = a - bi = Ae^{-j\theta}.$$
