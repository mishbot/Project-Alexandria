>[!note] Covariance
>The covariance of two random variables $X$ and $Y$ is defined as
>$$\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=\mathbb{E}[(X-\upmu_X)(Y-\upmu_Y)]=\mathbb{E}[XY]-\mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y],$$
# Explanation.
Remember the proof of the [[3.3b Linearity of Variance|linearity of variance]], where we canceled the term $2c_1c_2(\mathbb{E}[XY]-\mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y])$ because the two variables are independent (and therefore $\mathbb{E}[XY]=\mathbb{E}[X]\mathbb{E}[Y]$)? Well, this turns out to be the definition of covariance.

# Properties
#### (*Bilinearity*) $\text{Cov}(\alpha X,\beta Y)=\alpha\beta\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)$.
#### (*Reflexivity*) $\operatorname{Cov}(X,X)=\operatorname{Var}(X)$.

#### (*Independence*) If $X,Y$ are independent, then $\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)=0$.
