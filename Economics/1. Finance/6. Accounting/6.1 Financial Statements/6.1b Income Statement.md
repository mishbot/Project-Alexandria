>[!note] Income Statement
>The **income statement** is a financial report that summarizes a company’s revenues, expenses, and net profit over a specific period. It helps assess a company’s profitability and operational efficiency. In essence, a company's income statement must equal the formula 
>$$\text{Net Income} = \text{Revenue} - \text{Expenses}.$$
>The following example shows the trickle-down equation from revenue to net income:
>```tikz
>\usepackage{tikz}
>\usepackage{amsmath}
>
>\begin{document}
>
>\begin{tikzpicture}
>    % Draw table borders
>    \draw (0,-0.55) -- (10,-0.55);
>    \draw (0,-3.05) -- (10,-3.05);
>    \draw (0,-7.05) -- (10,-7.05);
>    \draw (0,-7.15) -- (10,-7.15);
>
>
>    % Left column text
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,0.2) {\textbf{NET SALES}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-0.3) {\textbf{COST OF GOODS SOLD}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-0.8) {\textbf{GROSS INCOME}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-1.8) {\textbf{SALES \& MARKETING}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-2.3) {\textbf{RESEARCH \& DEVELOPMENT}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-2.8) {\textbf{GENERAL \& ADMINISTRATIVE}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-3.3) {\textbf{OPERATING EXPENSES}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-4.3) {\textbf{INCOME FROM OPERATIONS}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-5.3) {\textbf{INTEREST INCOME}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-5.8) {\textbf{INCOME TAXES}};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (0.2,-6.8) {\textbf{NET INCOME}};
>
>    % Right column values
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,0.2) {A};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-0.2) {B};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-0.8) {A - B = C};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-1.8) {D};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-2.3) {E};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-2.8) {F};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-3.3) {D + E + F = G};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-4.3) {C - G = H};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-5.3) {I};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-5.8) {J};
>    \node[anchor=west] at (7,-6.8) {H + I - J = K};
>\end{tikzpicture}
>
>\end{document}
>```
>The bottom line (i.e., net income) of the income statement flows into the [[6.1a Balance Sheet|balance sheet]] under shareholders’ equity.
>
>>[!warning] Revenue $\neq$ Sales
>>Revenue is total income generated from all business activities, including sales.
