>[!note] CSP Ordering
>Ordering heuristics improve CSP search efficiency by deciding **which variable to assign next** and **which value to try first**. Instead of blindly picking, good ordering strategies reduce the chances of dead-ends and cut down on backtracking using two broad principles:
>- **Minimum Remaining Values (MRV)** chooses the most constrained variable to assign next.
>⠀
>- **Least Constraining Value (LCV)** selects the value for the chosen variable that rules out the fewest possibilities in the domains of its neighboring variables, preserving the most flexibility for future assignments.
>
