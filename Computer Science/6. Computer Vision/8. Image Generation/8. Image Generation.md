The space of all images is enormous. A uniform random sample from it, such as the $100 \times 100$ color image `np.random.rand(100, 100, 3)`, looks like noise. Meaningful images occupy a thin subset of this space, often described as a manifold of natural images. Image generation methods model that subset and produce new points on it, starting either from noise or from an input image. This section covers the computer-vision methods, from statistical texture models through image translation with GANs to editing with a pretrained flow model.

- [[9.3. Texture Histograms]], for marginal and joint texture statistics.
- [[5.2.4. CNNs]], for feature maps and pretrained networks.
- [[10.1. Generative Adversarial Networks]], for the adversarial game behind pix2pix and CycleGAN.
- [[6.4. U-Net]], for the encoder-decoder used in image-to-image networks.
- [[10.3.2. Flow Matching]] and [[10.3.2. Classifier-Free Guidance]], for sampling and guidance in flow models.

## Contents

1. [[8.1. Parametric Texture Synthesis]]: start from noise and match texture statistics, from Heeger and Bergen's pyramid histograms to Gatys's CNN Gram matrices.
2. [[8.2. Neural Style Transfer]]: render a photo's content in an artwork's style with a content loss and a Gram-matrix style loss.
3. [[8.3. Image Colorization]]: predict $ab$ color from lightness; why L2 regression gives dull colors and how classification fixes it.
4. [[8.4. Pix2Pix]]: image-to-image translation from paired data with a conditional GAN and an L1 term.
5. [[8.5. CycleGAN]]: translation between unpaired collections with two generators and a cycle-consistency loss.
6. [[8.6. Flow-Based Image Editing]]: SDEdit-style editing, text-guided edits, visual anagrams and hybrid images with a pretrained flow model.

## Recommended Path

Read [[8.1. Parametric Texture Synthesis]] first, since its picture of noise moved onto a set of acceptable images returns in [[8.6. Flow-Based Image Editing]]. Read [[8.3. Image Colorization]] before [[8.4. Pix2Pix]], because pix2pix answers the loss-design problem that colorization raises. [[8.5. CycleGAN]] builds directly on pix2pix.
