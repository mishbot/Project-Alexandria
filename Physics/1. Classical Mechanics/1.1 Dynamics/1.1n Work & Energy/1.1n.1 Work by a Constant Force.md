>[!note] Work Done by a Constant Force
>The work (in joules [$J = N \cdot m$]) done by a constant force on an object is defined to be the product of the magnitude of the displacement times the component of the force parallel to the displacement. In equation form, we can write
>$$W=\vec{F}_{||}|\vec{d}|=|\vec{F}||\vec{d}|\cos\theta,$$
>where $\theta$ is the angle at which the constant force $\vec{F}$ does work on the object.
