>[!note] Power
>Power (SI: $W$/$J \cdot s$) is defined as the rate at which work is done. It quantifies how quickly energy is converted from one form to another or how fast work is performed. Average power equals the work done divided by the time it takes to do it, such that
>$$\bar{P}=\frac{W}{t} = \frac{E}{t}.$$
>Then, instantaneous power can be defined as
>$$P = \frac{d}{dt}W = \frac{d}{dt}E.$$
>It is often convenient to define power in terms of force. That is, for $W=\int^{b}_{a}\vec{F}d\vec{\ell},$
>$$P = \frac{d}{dt}\vec{F}d\vec{\ell} = \vec{F}\vec{v}.$$
