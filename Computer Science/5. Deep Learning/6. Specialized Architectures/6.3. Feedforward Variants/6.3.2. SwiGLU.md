> [!note] SwiGLU
> **SwiGLU** is a [[6.3.1. GLU|GLU-family ]] feed-forward variant where the gating nonlinearity is **SiLU** (also called Swish) rather than sigmoid. The typical Transformer FFN parameterization uses two "up" projections from $d_{\text{model}}$ to $d_{\text{ff}}$: one is passed through SiLU to become the gate, and the other provides the value stream. Their elementwise product is then projected back down:
> $$\mathrm{SwiGLU}(x)= W_2\Big(\mathrm{SiLU}(W_1x)\ \odot\ (W_3x)\Big).$$
> Relative to sigmoid-gated GLU, SiLU gating is smooth and does not strictly saturate into a hard $(0,1)$ mask, which can yield better gradient flow and empirical performance in modern LLMs. In practice, SwiGLU is usually paired with a larger hidden dimension than the classic ReLU/GELU FFN so that the gated product retains sufficient capacity, making it a common default choice in LLaMA-style decoder blocks.
