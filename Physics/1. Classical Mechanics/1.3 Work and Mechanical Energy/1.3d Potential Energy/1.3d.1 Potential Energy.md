>[!note] Potential Energy
>**Potential energy** (SI: Joule / $\text{J} \equiv \text{kg} \cdot \text{m}^2 \cdot \text{s}^{-2}$) is a scalar quantity that denotes how much work a system can release or absorb due to the relative positions of its parts. 
>
>It is defined for systems where internal forces do work that depends only on the initial and final positions–and not on the path taken–called **conservative forces**, for which it holds that
>$$\oint \vec{F} \cdot d\vec{r} = 0.$$
>
>Mathematically, the change in potential energy is defined as the negative of the work done by conservative forces, where
>$$\Delta U = -W_{\text{ext}}.$$
# Intuition
Potential energy is introduced a configuration-based quantity that depends on the relative position of interacting objects. Consider the gravitational interaction between two objects, say Earth and an apple. There are **two perspectives**:
1. **External Force View (apple only)**
	- Gravity is an external force.
    - The work done by gravity leads to a change in kinetic energy, such that $\Delta K = W_{\text{ext}}$.
2. **System View (apple + Earth)**
    - Gravity is internal to the system.
    - No external work is done, so the total mechanical energy stays constant, such that $\Delta K + \Delta U = 0 \implies \Delta K = -\Delta U$.

From both, we conclude that $\Delta U = -W_{\text{ext}}$.
