>[!note] Degrees of Freedom
>The **degrees of freedom (DoFs)** of a system are the minimum number of independent parameters required to uniquely specify its configuration.
>- A point in $\mathbb{R}^3$ has 3 DoFs.
>- A rigid body in $\mathbb{R}^3$ has 6 DoFs (three translational and three rotational).
>- A serial robot with $n$ independent joints has $n$ internal DoFs.
>
>The robot’s configuration space therefore has dimension $n$, whereas its workspace has dimension at most 6. When $n>6$, the robot is redundant: internal DoFs exceed task-space DoFs.
>
>In dynamic models, the number of state variables is typically twice the number of DoFs, since both configuration variables and their associated velocities must be represented.
