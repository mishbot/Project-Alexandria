>[!note] Transfer Function
>A **transfer function** is a mathematical representation that describes the input-output relationship of a system in the frequency domain, providing its frequency response. For a system $X(j\omega)$, 
>$$H(j\omega) = \frac{X_\text{out}(j\omega)}{X_\text{in}(j\omega)} = |H(j\omega)|e^{j\omega \phi},$$
>where by definition $|H(j\omega)|$ is the system's gain (or magnitude), and $\phi(j\omega) = \tan^{-1}\frac{\mathrm{Im}\{H(\omega)\}}{\mathrm{Re}\{H(\omega)\}}$ is the system's phase response.
