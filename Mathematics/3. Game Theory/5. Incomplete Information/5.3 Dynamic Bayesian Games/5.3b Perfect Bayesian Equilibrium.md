>[!note] 
>A **Perfect Bayesian Equilibrium** of a [[3.1 Extensive Form Game|dynamic]] [[5.1  Bayesian Game|Bayesian game]] is a strategy profile in which each type $t_i \in T_i$ of every player $i \in N$ is a [[2.2a Best Response|best response]] to the strategies of all other players given $i$'s beliefs $t_i$ about $t_{-i}$.
