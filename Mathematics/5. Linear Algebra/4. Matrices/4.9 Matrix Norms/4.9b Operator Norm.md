>[!note] Operator Norm
>The $\ell^p$-induced **operator norm** of a matrix $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$, given a [[3.5b p-Norm|vector p-norm]],  is the maximum normalized amount by which $A$ can stretch any nonzero vector, such that
>$$\|A\|_{p} = \max\limits_{\vec{x} \neq 0}\frac{\|A\vec{x}\|_{p}}{\|\vec{x}\|_{p}} = \max\limits_{\|\vec{x}\|_{p} \leq 1}\|A\vec{x}\|_{p},$$
>where the latter comes from the fact that the operator norm measures the magnitude relative to the unit norm (it is normalized). Then:
>- The $\ell^1$-induced norm is the largest absolute column sum, $\|A\|_{1}=\max\limits_{j=1,\ldots,n}\sum\limits^{m}_{i=1}|A_{ij}|$.
>- The $\ell^\infty$-induced  norm is the largest absolute row sum, $\|A\|_{\infty}=\max\limits_{i=1,\ldots,m}\sum\limits^{n}_{j=1}|A_{ij}|$.
>- The $\ell^2$-induced (spectral) norm corresponds to the square root of the largest eigenvalue of $A^TA$, such that $\|A\|_{2}=\sqrt{ \max\limits_{i=1,\ldots,n}\lambda_{i} }$.

# Properties
#### (*Submultiplication*) $\|AB\|_{p} \leq \|A\|_{p}\|B\|_{p}$
