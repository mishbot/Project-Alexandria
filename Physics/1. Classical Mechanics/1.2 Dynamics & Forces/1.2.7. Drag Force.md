>[!note] Drag Force
>**Drag force** is the resistive force experienced by an object moving through a fluid (such as air or water). It always acts in the direction **opposite** to the object’s velocity relative to the fluid and depends on the **speed**, **shape**, and **surface properties** of the object, as well as the **viscosity** of the fluid.
>
>When the relative speed is small and the fluid flow is smooth (laminar), the drag force is approximately **proportional** to the object’s speed, such that
>$$\vec{F}_{\text{drag}} = -b \vec{v},$$
>where $b$ is a positive constant depending on the fluid’s viscosity and the object’s geometry. The negative sign indicates that drag opposes motion.
>
>When the object moves faster and the flow becomes more **turbulent**, the drag force becomes roughly **proportional to the square of the speed**, such that $\vec{F}_{\text{drag}} \propto -v^2$.
