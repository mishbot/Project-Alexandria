>[!note] Measures of Bond Risk
>[[2.1a.2 Yield Curves|Credit spread]] measures the additional risk of a fixed-income security compared to a risk-free benchmark. Aside from the trivial value $x=y-y_{0}$, where $y_{0}$ is the yield of a risk-free bond, there are multiple other ways to measure spread:
>- **Static spread** is the constant premium added to the risk-free spot rate. This is the same as $x$ for stripped bonds, but varies for coupon bonds.
>- **Option-adjusted spread (OAS)** is the static spread adjusted for embedded options by removing the value of optionality, reflecting only the credit and liquidity risks (i.e., the credit spread of a callable bond adjusted for the call provision).
