>[!note] Resistor
>A resistor is a [[2.1 Circuit Components|circuit component]] that represents any material tending to resist the flow of current. It follows Ohm's Law, or $V=IR$.
>```tikz
>\usepackage{circuitikz}
>\begin{document}
>\begin{circuitikz}[american voltages]
>  \node at (0,3.75) {Symbol};
>  \draw
>    (0,0) to [R, v<=$V_{elem}$, i<=$I_{elem}$, l=$R$] (0,3);
\end{circuitikz}
\hspace{1cm}
\begin{tikzpicture}[domain=0:4]
  \node at (0,2.25) {IV Relationship};
  \draw[<->] (-1.5,0)--(1.5,0) node[below]{$V_{elem}$};
  \draw[<->] (0,-1.5)--(0,1.5) node[left]{$I_{elem}$};
  \draw[<->, color=red] (-1,-1)--(1,1);
\end{tikzpicture}
\hspace{1cm}
\begin{tikzpicture}
  \node at (0,2.25) {$V_{elem}=I_{elem}R$};
  \node at (0,1.75) {$I_{elem}=\frac{V{elem}}{R}$};
  \node at (0,0){};
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
>```
>The SI unit of resistance is called the Ohm ($\Omega$). Resistance can alternatively be calculated using the formula
>$$R = \frac{\ell}{\sigma A},$$
>where $\ell$ is the length of the object, $A$ is its cross-sectional area, $\sigma$ is its resistivity, and $\rho = \frac{1}{\sigma}$ is its conductivity:
>```tikz
>\usepackage{tikz}
>\usetikzlibrary{3d,calc}
>
>\begin{document}
>
>\begin{tikzpicture}[scale=0.75]
>
>    % Parameters
>    \def\R{0.75} % Radius of the cylinder (small size)
>    \def\L{12}   % Length of the cylinder (long size)
>    \def\A{0.25} % Opacity for transparency effect
>    
>    % Draw the back face of the cylinder
>    \shade[ball color=blue!30, opacity=\A] (\L,0) circle (\R); % Back circle
>    \draw[dashed] (\L,0) circle (\R); % Dashed line for transparency
>    
>    % Draw the sides of the cylinder
>    \draw[fill=blue!30, opacity=\A] (0,-\R) -- (\L,-\R) arc[start angle=-90, end angle=90, radius=\R] -- (0,\R) arc[start angle=90, end angle=-90, radius=\R];
>    
>    % Draw the front face of the cylinder
>    \shade[ball color=blue!30, opacity=\A] (0,0) circle (\R); % Front circle
>    \draw (0,0) circle (\R); % Front edge
>    
>    % Draw the wires connected to the cylinder
>    \draw[thick] (-1.5,0) -- (0,0); % Left wire
>    \draw[thick] (\L,0) -- (\L+1.5,0); % Right wire
>    
>    % Labels for the cylinder
>    \node at (\L/2, 0) {\huge $\sigma$}; % Sigma inside cylinder
>    \draw[<->, thick] (-0.75,-2*\R) -- (\L+0.75,-2*\R) node[midway, below]{\huge $\ell$}; % Length indicator arrow across entire diagram
>    
>    % Label for the cross-sectional area A
>    \draw[->, thick] (\L,0) -- ++(1.5,0);
>    \node at (\L,0) {\huge $A$}; % Label for cross-sectional area
>    
>\end{tikzpicture}
>
>\end{document}
>```
>The SI unit for conductance is the Siemen (S), given by $G = R^{-1}$.
