>[!note] Kirchhoff's Voltage Law
>**Kirchhoff's voltage law** (KCL) states that net voltage flowing out of (or into) any closed loop must equal zero.
>```tikz
\usepackage[straightvoltages]{circuitikz}
\begin{document}
\begin{circuitikz}
\draw 
(-2.2,0) to[generic, v^=$v_1$] (0,0) 
to [generic, v^=$v_2$] (2.2,0) 
(0,-2.2) to[generic, v=$v_3$] (0,0); 
\end{circuitikz}
\end{document}
>```
>In other words,
>$$v_1-v_2+v_3=0,$$
>where $v_2$ is flowing out of the node and is therefore subracted.
