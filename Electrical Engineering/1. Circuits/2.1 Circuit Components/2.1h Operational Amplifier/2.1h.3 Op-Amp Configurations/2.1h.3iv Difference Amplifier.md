>[!note] Difference Amplifier
>A difference amplifier is an [[2.1h.1 Ideal Op-Amp|op-amp]] configuration that subtracts two voltages in addition to adding a gain to each of them, such that $V_{o}=G_{1}V_{1}+G_{2}V_{2}$, where $G_{1}=-\frac{R_{f}}{R_{1}}$ and $G_{2}=\left(\frac{R_{4}}{R_{3}+R_{4}}\right)\left(\frac{R_{1}+R_{2}}{R_{1}}\right)$.
