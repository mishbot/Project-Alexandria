>[!note] Average Return
>**Average return** is a measure of the central tendency of returns over a period of time, representing the typical or mean performance of an investment or asset. For returns $R_{t}, R_{t+1}, \ldots, R_{t+n}$, the ***arithmetic average return*** is given as
>$$\bar{R}_{\text{A}}=\frac{1}{n}\sum^{n}_{i=0}R_{t+i},$$
>whereas the ***geometric average return*** is given as
>$$\bar{R}_{\text{G}}=\left(1+\prod^{n}_{i=1}R_{t+i}\right)^\frac{1}{n}.$$
