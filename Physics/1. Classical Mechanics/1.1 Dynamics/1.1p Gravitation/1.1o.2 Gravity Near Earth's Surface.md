>[!note] Gravity at Earth's Surface
>From Newton's [[law of universal gravitation|1.1o.1 Law of Universal Gravitation]] gravity at Earth surface can be derived as
>$$g=G \frac{m_E}{r_E^2},$$
>where $m_E$ is Earth's mass, and $r_E$ is the radius of Earth's surface (i.e. the distance of the object from the Earth's center).
