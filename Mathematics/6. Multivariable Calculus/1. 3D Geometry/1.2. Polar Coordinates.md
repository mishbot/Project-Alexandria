>[!note] Polar Coordinates
>Polar coordinates represent points in a 2D plane using a **distance** and an **angle** rather than Cartesian $(x, y)$. Each point is defined by its **radius** $r > 0$ (distance from origin) and **angle** $\theta \in [0, 2\pi)$ (measured from the positive x-axis), such that
>$$r=\sqrt{ x^2 + y^2 }, \quad \theta = \tan^{-1} x / y.$$
>Conversely, one can go from polar to Cartesian coordinates, such that
>$$x = r \cos \theta, \quad y = r \sin \theta.$$
