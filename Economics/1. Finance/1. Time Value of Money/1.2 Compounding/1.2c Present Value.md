>[!note] Present Value
>The present value is the current worth of a [[1.2a Compounding|compounded]] sum of money or stream of cash flows, given a specific rate of return $r$, and a time period of $t$ years over $n$ periods. It is calculated by discounting the future amount(s) back to the present using the formula
>$$\text{PV} = \frac{\text{FV}}{(1+\frac{r}{n})^{nt}}.$$
>
>Given multiple cashflows $C_{1}, \ldots, C_{N}$, the **net future value** of the cashflows is calculated by compounding each cashflow to a common future date using the appropriate interest rate, and then summing these compounded amounts. Mathematically,
>$$\text{NPV} = \sum^{nt}_{i=0}\frac{C_{i}}{\left( 1+\frac{r}{n} \right)^{i}}.$$
