>[!note] Newton's Second Law
>Newton’s second law of motion states that the acceleration of an object is directly proportional to the net force acting on the object and inversely proportional to its mass. In simple terms, this law describes how the force applied to an object causes it to accelerate. 
>
>Mathematically, Newton's second law can be expressed as
>$$\sum\vec{F} = m\vec{a},$$
>where $\sum\vec{F}$ is the net force of an object, $m$ is its mass, and $\vec{a}$ is its acceleration.

# Properties
#### (*Inverse Ratio*) $\sum\vec{F}_1=\sum\vec{F}_2 \implies \frac{m_2}{m_1}=\frac{a_2}{a_1}$
