>[!note] Fenchel Duality
>**Fenchel duality** is a framework in convex analysis that creates a [[4.1. Duality|dual optimization problem]] using the concept of [[2.3 Convex Conjugates|convex (Fenchel) conjugates]]. Let $f: X \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty \}$, $g: Y \to \mathbb{R} \cup \{ +\infty \}$, and $A: X \to Y$ be a linear map. Consider the primal problem
>$$p^* = \inf_{x \in X} \{ f(x)+g(Ax) \}.$$
>Then, the **Fenchel dual** is given as 
>$$d^* = \sup_{y^* \in Y^*}\{ -f^*(A^Ty^*)-g^*(-y^*) \}.$$

Consider the perturbation function $F(x,y)=f(x)+g(Ax-y)$. Then, the primal problem is given on the function $F(x,0)$. Taking the convex conjugate of $F(x,0)$, we get:
