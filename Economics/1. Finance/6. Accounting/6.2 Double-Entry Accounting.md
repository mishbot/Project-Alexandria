>[!note] Double-Entry Accounting
>Double-entry accounting is an accounting system where every financial transaction is recorded in at least two accounts, ensuring that the accounting equation remains balanced. Each **journal entry** has two equal and opposite effects:
>1. A **debit** (Dr.) – An increase in assets or expenses, or a decrease in liabilities, equity, or revenue.
>2. A **credit** (Cr.) – An increase in liabilities, equity, or revenue, or a decrease in assets or expenses.
