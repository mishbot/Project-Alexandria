- **Low Rank Approximation (LoRA)** injects low-rank trainable matrices into selected weight projections while freezing the original parameters. For a weight matrix $W \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times k}$, LoRA decomposes the update into a rank-$r$ factorization:
>$$W' = W + \Delta W, \qquad \Delta W = B A, \quad B \in \mathbb{R}^{d \times r}, \; A \in \mathbb{R}^{r \times k},$$
>with $r \ll \min(d, k)$. Only $A$ and $B$ are optimized, yielding a parameter cost of $O(r(d+k))$, a dramatic reduction compared to full fine-tuning. LoRA preserves the forward pass semantics of the pretrained model while enabling efficient task adaptation. It is inserted in attention projections (typically $W_Q, W_V$), or MLP layers.
