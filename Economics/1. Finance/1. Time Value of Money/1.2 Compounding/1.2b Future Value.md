>[!note] Future Value
>The **future value** of an investment is the amount of money it is expected to [[1.2a Compounding|compound]] over $t$ years, $n$ times per year, at a given APR $r$. Formally,
>$$\text{FV} = \left( 1+\frac{r}{n} \right)^{nt} \cdot \text{PV}.$$
>
>Given multiple cashflows $C_{1}, \ldots, C_{N}$, the **net future value** of the cashflows is calculated by compounding each cash flow to a common future date using the appropriate interest rate, and then summing these compounded amounts. Mathematically,
>$$\text{NFV} = \sum^{nt}_{i=1}\left(1+\frac{r}{n}\right)^{nt-i} C_{i}.$$
