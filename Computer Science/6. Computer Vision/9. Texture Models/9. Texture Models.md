Texture models describe an image region by statistics of local features pooled over the region, instead of by pixel values at fixed positions. This section starts from human perception, with Julesz's textons and preattentive vision, and models early vision with banks of oriented filters. It then summarizes filter outputs with histograms and clusters them into a dictionary of visual words, a general tool for recognizing textures and scene categories. Generating new texture from the same statistics is covered in [[8.1. Parametric Texture Synthesis]].

## Contents

- [[9.1. Texture]]: texture as repeating "stuff", instance vs. category recognition, preattentive vision, and the Julesz conjecture on first-order and second-order statistics.
