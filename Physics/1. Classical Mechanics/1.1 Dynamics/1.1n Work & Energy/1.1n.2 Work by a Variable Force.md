>[!note] Work Done by a Variable Force
>The [[1.1n.1 Work by a Constant Force|work]] done by a variable force in moving an object between two points is equal to the area under the versus  curve between those two points, such that for a $2$-dimensional space with points $a$ and $b$,
>$$W=\int^{b}_{a}\vec{F} \cdot d\vec{\ell}=\int^{x_b}_{x_a}F_xdx + \int^{y_b}_{y_a}F_ydx + \int^{z_b}_{z_a}F_zdz,$$
>where $d\vec{\ell}$ is the vector of the object's infinitesimal displacement.
